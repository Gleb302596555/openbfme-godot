## Two ring-recipient declarations

`object/evilfaction/units/mordor/mordorsauron.ini:2,1166,5734` declares `MordorNekromantSauron`, `MordorGorthaur`, and `MordorSauron_mod`. The former lists **eight** synchronized names; the latter **ten**. Their `ONE_RING` conditions and death OCLs require both upgrades (`mordorsauron.ini:1041–1057,2316–2334`); generic gating is bounded in `PORT238_FORTRESS_TRACE.md`.

In particular, both synchronized lists include `SpecialAbilityMachtderVerdammten`, while `SpecialAbilityEwigeKnechtschaft` is listed only by the Gorthaur → Sauron toggle, even though all three objects directly declare the two powers. `PORT-238 Necromancer and Gorthaur-style ring delivery*` reproduces both name lists in a synthetic fixture, with simplified special-power modules for only these two powers. On direct delivery it checks both object upgrades, ring destruction, friendly replacement, source removal and the copied Might timer; Eternal copies for Gorthaur but not for Necromancer. On matching isolated core/headers `3f0105c8` plus the separately compiled upstream PORT-286 parser: **66/66 assertions**, and the isolated `PORT-238*` suite **41/41, 2767/2767**. Removing Might only from both synthetic timer lists yields **64/66** (fixture sensitivity control, not pre-fix engine control). RW contain `0x8840FC`, auto-command `0x8B8E2E`, toggle `0x8B1690` and named timer loop `0x8B12BF` are traced in `PORT238_RW_ANALYSIS.md` / `PORT238_BOROMIR_TRACE.md`. The remaining timer names, live modules, options, loading, unit selection and real state transfer are untested; S-7321, S-7446 and S-861 remain.

`MordorAnnatar` also declares a toggle targeting `MordorSauron_mod` (`:5474–5501`) but its ring-grant route and command activation are not resolved here. `MordorSauron_mod` has an ungated ring death OCL (`:7076–7083`); its separate `_RingHero` child (`:7183`) declares Mordor-faction-triggered removal of both OBJECT ring upgrades across the owner's objects and another ring OCL (`:7243–7253`). ### Bounded synthetic Athelas exercise

`PORT-238 Sauron-mod style Athelas*` first creates a wounded nearby friendly hero, then a synthetic two-AutoHeal caster. Without a button use, its `StartsActive` / `SingleBurst` medicine healer recovers 5000 of the ally's health; the ally is damaged again, along with the caster and three other nearby/far objects. Three frames later caster and ally remain wounded; a **direct** `SpecialAbilityUpdate` invocation of `SPECIAL_ATHELAS` reactivates the first radius-10, `KindOf HERO`, 5000-point medicine healer. Caster and friendly nearby hero each recover 5000 while enemy, friendly non-hero and far hero do not. The later passive AutoHeal has amount 1 and a long start delay. This is **40/40 assertions** in the matching isolated runner, suite **42/42 cases and 2807/2807 assertions**. Changing only the synthetic medicine `StartsActive` to No fails at the first-heal assertion (**24/25**, early REQUIRE). Before adding the initial-wounded scenario, swapping the order of the two synthetic AutoHeal declarations failed two later health checks (**36/38**). These are fixture sensitivity controls, not evidence of a port fix.

Port `SpecialAbilityUpdate` looks up `AutoHealBehavior` by name (`SpecialAbilityModules.cpp`); RW `0x854841` performs a lookup and calls `0x855431`. RW constructor `0x85562D` seeds the waiting flag from `ButtonTriggered` and activates on `StartsActive`; update begins `0x8558C0`. The synthetic fixture has no actual Sauron model, upgrades/level admission, command button, FX or effective inheritance; exact scheduler behavior under real creation, healing suppression and off-map filter (S-858), and real power timing remain unverified.
