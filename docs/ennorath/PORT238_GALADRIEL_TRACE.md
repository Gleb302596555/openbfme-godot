Accept's `DoCommandUpgrade` (`:897–901`) requires **all** of PLAYER `Upgrade_GaladrielRingannehmen` and both OBJECT ring upgrades; its unpaused toggle targets `LothlorienGaladrielRingannehmen`. Reject's `DoCommandUpgrade` (`:926–929`) names only PLAYER `Upgrade_GaladrielRingablehnen`, and its toggle targets `LothlorienGaladrielRingablehnen`. Both list four synchronized timer names (`:877–924`). The separate `LothlorienGaladrielNachRing` has a direct accept toggle and a two-ring-upgrade trigger with no explicit `RequiresAllTriggers` (`:4294–4316`), so it must not be equated with the base's three-way condition. The source's `Upgrade_HasSarumanonOrthanc` selects the base's after-ring command set (`:937–940`), conflicting with its decision set. Trigger provenance and all other routes to these forms remain open. RW generic upgrade mux `0x8D26F3`, `DoCommandUpgrade` `0x8B8E2E`, and toggle replacement `0x8B1690 -> 0x8B140D` are implemented in `UpgradeModuleClasses.cpp` / `SpecialAbilityModules.cpp`; S-7321, S-7446, S-861 and S-7773 remain.

`PORT-238 Galadriel-style ring choice*` uses direct synthetic PLAYER upgrade grants, ring delivery and two replacement objects. Nine subcases cover delivery without a choice, accept before/after delivery, accept with just either OBJECT upgrade, reject after delivery, synthetic reject without delivery, and the prior flag before/after delivery. The prior flag selects a different command-set **name**; the other player's synthetic hero remains on its base set. It passes **316/316 assertions** on matching isolated `3f0105c8` core/headers with the upstream PORT-286 parser compilation unit; the full isolated `PORT-238*` suite passes **27/27 tests, 1649/1649 assertions**. Changing only the accept fixture to `RequiresAllTriggers = No` fails 9 checks (256/265, with fatal checks shortening the run); removing the synthetic `ConflictsWith` fails 4/316 checks. These modify fixture data, not engine code, and are sensitivity controls rather than code-fix controls. The reject-without-delivery case establishes only what happens after an artificial direct PLAYER grant: the real purchase button may be inaccessible then. The test does not click purchase buttons, use actual real-data templates, verify four timer values, simultaneous contradictory choices, death/drop, HUD or gameplay.

## Telperion: what the matrix can and cannot establish

The literal weapon (`includes/weapon.inc:35206–35256`) has three radius-170 `AttributeModifierNugget`s (a named troll filter, `NONE +HERO`, then an unfiltered debuff), a radius-170 `ParalyzeNugget` (`ALL -HERO -STRUCTURE` and two named exceptions, 12000 ms, `FreezeAnimation = No`, `AffectHordeMembers = Yes`), and a 2200 FLAME radius-170 `DamageNugget` with `DamageScalar = 0% ALL -HERO`. Its relation mask is `ENEMIES NEUTRALS NOT_SIMILAR`. The stone list uses `SPELL_SUNFLARE_DURATION` = 20000 ms (`_gamedata.inc:9496`); the hero and general debuffs last 13000 and 12000 ms. RW generic damage filling is at `0x90E28C`, modifier and paralysis application at `0x90EAF9` and `0x90F177`; these addresses concern generic mechanisms, not a captured Ennorath game.

The older `73be08aa` matrix row reported that the command fired but recorded a biggest hit of zero and a zero nominal amount against that recorded victim. The DAMAGE probe in `engine/tests/SweepEffectProbe.h` judges tracked hits against per-victim nominal damage; the result does **not** independently assert that all modifier and paralysis effects are absent. Current `test_hud_factb_units.cpp` contains a LOCAL-113 retry for zero-nominal targetless AOE, including a hero and infantry scene. The old row alone does not establish which retry, victim, or effects occurred. The later generated matrix merged from main `adbccb1f` reports **PASS**: it records the power firing after 70 frames, a first hit of 822 against nominal 770 and `ErinnerunganTelperionDebuff` on one object; the stored description ends mid-field. That is an upstream automated observation, not a rerun by this trace and not proof of every nugget, target or real gameplay. No matrix file was manually reclassified.

`PORT-238 Galadriel-style Telperion weapon sweeps damage modifiers and paralysis` directly starts a synthetic WeaponFire ability and tests synthetic enemies (hero, infantry and a name-filtered troll), allied hero and out-of-range enemy hero. It observes the nearby enemy hero lose 2200 health, infantry/troll lose none; hero retains the longer hero debuff, infantry receives the general debuff, the troll receives both stone and general lists, infantry/troll are user-paralyzed and the hero, ally and distant hero are not. Allied and far heroes do not receive the hero debuff. This passes **53/53 assertions**, on matching isolated `3f0105c8` core/headers plus upstream PORT-286 parser; `PORT-238*` is **28/28 tests, 1702/1702 assertions**. A sensitivity control deleting only the synthetic `DamageScalar` yields **51/53**, failing the infantry and troll health checks; it is a fixture control, not an engine code-fix control.

This is not the mod's loaded weapon or actual troll list; the fixture uses a synthetic troll name, `NoArmor`, `IgnoreFacingCheck`, direct ability initiation and a pre-existing target scene. It does not test neutral, structures, named exceptions, horde members, expiry, actual effects of the modifiers, target acquisition/autoability, starter/level gate, animations, UI or a real game. Category replacement, filters, timing and shared implementations still require direct RW and current-main confirmation; do not infer full parity from this sweep.
