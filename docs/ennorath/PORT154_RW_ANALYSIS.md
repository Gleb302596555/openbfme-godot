## Scope and stops

Guard* (`+0x2C..+0x38`) are parsed and copied/xferred, but the RotWK guard-range path RW 0x949567 -> RW 0x6FF412 does not read them: do not add guard scaling or a test that encodes it. `AICrushesInfantry` has a real generic attack-condition reader (RW 0x745901); its common transition family is outside PORT-154 and remains S-325, a separate task. `AIDozerBoredRadiusModifier` has a real reader RW 0x88BED2, with typed vtable slot as above, but its runtime consumer is not established and the bored wander is already S-1282. This PR changes only the research record and `docs/STOPS.md`; no engine or test change is claimed. Adjacent MinClumpDensity has no found behavioural reader; RotateSkirmishBases is parsed-unused by the bounded dead-function proof above. **WallHeight remains a research stop**, not a claim of unused: a later/escaped AIData pointer could evade the 14-instruction window. A follow-up must show an owned `TheAI+0x18 -> +0x48` behavioural reader or expand the bounded absence proof before assigning WallHeight a used/unused verdict.
