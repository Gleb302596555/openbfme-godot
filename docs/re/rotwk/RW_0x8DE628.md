## Notes on the OpenBFME port

- `UnitVoiceResponse::moveRequiresClimbingWalls` (`UnitVoiceResponse.cpp`) follows steps 1–4 above,
  querying `world->pathfinder().quickDoesPathExist(hordeAi->adapter(), horde->getPosition(), &destPos, nullptr)`
  and then re-querying with `PathfindLocomotorInfo noScale = hordeAi->locomotorInfo(); noScale.crusher = false;`.
- `LocomotorSet::addLocomotor` (`RW 0x5E80F9 .. 0x5E8102`) maps `LocomotorTemplate::m_scalesWalls`
  (`+ 0x150`) to `LocomotorSet + 0x15`, which `PathfindMovement` (`RW 0x6EA06D`) copies into
  movement `+ 0xC` (`PathfindLocomotorInfo::crusher`, `AIUpdateInterface::locomotorInfo()`). Passing
  `&noScale` with `crusher = false` reproduces retail's cloned non-scaling `LocomotorSet` without
  allocating temporary `LocomotorTemplate` instances (`"TempVoiceMoveOverWallTesting_*"`) or
  touching simulation state.
- `UnitVoiceResponse::moveVoices` calls `moveRequiresClimbingWalls` for `VOICE_MOVE_OVER_WALLS` (`5`)
  and `VoiceEnterStateMoveOverWalls` (`29`) after `VoiceMoveWhileAttacking` and before `VoiceMove` /
  `VoiceEnterStateMove` when `!done(c) && hasPosition` and either `objectVoice` or `crowdVoice` holds
  a voice for that row.
- Client presentation only: `UnitVoiceResponse.cpp` is excluded from simulation arithmetic audit
  (`tools/sim/sim_policy.json`) and touches no simulation, `crc`, or `xfer` state.
- `S-704` (`docs/STOPS.md` and `UnitVoiceResponse::acceptanceStops()`) is narrowed to drop
  `VoiceMoveOverWalls (RW 0x8DE628)`.
