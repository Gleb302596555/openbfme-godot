## PORT-341: why the floor module state and the floor list are separated

The module never draws: it decides *whether* a record exists and hands five arguments over (RW 0x4CEAA0..0x4CEAB4:
StaticModelLODMode, ForceToBack, ModelName, weather texture). Everything after that call is the terrain visual's
bookkeeping. PORT-341 ported the first half (`W3DFloorDrawState` in `W3DTreeDraw.h`), and the second half is a
capacity / lifetime rule with no visible effect until a map reaches 450 floors, which no Ennorath map does — hence
a stop rather than a port.
